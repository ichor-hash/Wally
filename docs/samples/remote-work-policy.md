# Remote Work Policy

## Eligibility
All full-time employees who have completed their probation period may work remotely.

## Remote days
Employees may work remotely up to three days per week. Team leads can agree a fixed schedule with their team.

## Fully remote arrangements
Requests to work fully remote must be submitted to your manager and approved by a director. Exceptions for medical reasons are handled by People Operations.

## Equipment
Each remote employee receives an equipment stipend of 500 dollars per year for a desk, chair, monitor or headset. Receipts are submitted through the expense tool within 30 days.

## Working hours
Remote employees must be reachable between 10:00 and 16:00 local time and keep their calendar up to date.

## Security
Company laptops must use the VPN on public networks. Confidential documents may not be printed at home.
